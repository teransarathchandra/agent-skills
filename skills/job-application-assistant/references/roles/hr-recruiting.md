# Role profile: HR and recruiting

Load this when the person works in HR generalist, talent acquisition, recruiting, people operations or HR administration. Follow it for intake, evidence, CV shape, keywords and screening answers.

## Titles and adjacent roles

- Common titles: HR Generalist, HR Business Partner, HR Coordinator, Recruiter, Talent Acquisition Specialist, Sourcer, People Operations Specialist, HRIS Analyst, Learning and Development Coordinator.
- Realistic moves after a layoff: recruiter to talent acquisition partner, sourcer or recruitment consultant (agency); HR generalist to people operations, HR business partner or HRIS analyst; HR administrator to payroll or benefits; internal recruiter to employer branding or talent operations.
- Search the person's title plus adjacent ones. Agency and in-house recruiter ads differ: agency ads often stress targets and business development, in-house ads stress hiring manager partnership and process.

## Evidence sources

- Hiring metrics: roles filled per quarter, time to fill, time to hire, offer acceptance rate, source mix, quality of hire proxies.
- HR delivery: onboarding completion, retention or turnover trend, policy work, training programmes run, HR case volume.
- System records: ATS and HRIS exports (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, BambooHR), but only the aggregate numbers.
- Public: published job posts the person wrote, a redacted process document, an employer-branding piece. Private: candidate and employee names, CVs, salaries, performance or disciplinary cases, health or visa details. Examples must contain no candidate or employee personal data; use aggregate figures and stand-in roles.

## Intake questions

1. Which side of HR was the main work? (answer as it is: recruiting, generalist, HR operations, HRIS, learning, benefits)
2. Which direction do you want?
   a) **Same role and level (Recommended: strongest evidence)**
   b) In-house to agency, or the reverse
   c) Recruiting to talent operations or HR business partner
   d) Other: type it
3. Which results can you share as numbers? (pick any)
   a) **Roles filled and time to fill (Recommended: most asked for)**
   b) Offer acceptance or retention
   c) Process or tool improvements
   d) Other: type it
4. Which systems have you used? (answer as it is: names and years)
5. Do you hold a CIPD, SHRM or HRCI credential? (answer as it is: name and level, or none)

## CV shape

- Order: header, summary, skills, experience, certifications, education.
- Summary leads with years, the hiring volume or HR scope, the sectors and one measurable result.
- Bullets, action then result then how:
  - "Filled 45 engineering and sales roles a year for a 400-person fictional firm, cutting time to fill from 52 to 38 days."
  - "Rebuilt onboarding for 60 new starters in Workday, lifting day-30 completion to 98%."
  - "Introduced structured interview scorecards across 6 teams, raising offer acceptance to 85%."
- Use aggregate figures only. Never invent a number.

## Keywords and credentials

- Keywords from the ad: full-cycle recruiting, sourcing, stakeholder management, ATS, employee relations, onboarding, HRIS, employment law, compensation and benefits, DEI, workforce planning, Greenhouse, Workday.
- CIPD (cipd.org) qualifications are named in many UK, Irish and some Gulf and Asian HR ads. SHRM (shrm.org) offers SHRM-CP and SHRM-SCP, and many US HR ads mention them. HRCI (hrci.org) offers aPHR, PHR and SPHR, also seen in US ads. A pattern, not a rule; check the target ads and country.
- Employment law knowledge is country-specific; name the countries the person has worked in.

## Portfolio and work samples

- Not expected for HR. Recruiters sometimes show sourcing strings or a job-ad rewrite.
- Build any sample from a fictional role; never include real candidates or employee data.

## Screening questions

- "Years of recruiting or HR experience": count full-time work in the function.
- "Experience with Workday, Greenhouse, or similar": answer from `experience.items`.
- "Do you hold CIPD, SHRM-CP or similar?": answer true or false; "studying" only if enrolled.
- "Right to work in this country": a fact; answer as it is, from the answer bank.

## Answer-bank experience keys

Set `"role_family": "hr-recruiting"`. Collect lowercase keys with years or true/false: `full-cycle recruiting`, `sourcing`, `greenhouse`, `lever`, `workday`, `bamboohr`, `hris`, `employee relations`, `onboarding`, `payroll admin`, `employment law`, `cipd`, `shrm-cp`, `shrm-scp`, `aphr`, `phr`.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Naming candidates, employees or cases, even in anonymised detail that could identify them.
- Counting hires without the time frame or team size.
- Listing a credential from a country where the person has not worked without saying so.
- Duties-only bullets such as "managed the recruitment process".
