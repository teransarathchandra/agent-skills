# Role profile: finance and accounting

Load this when the person works in accounting, bookkeeping, finance, payroll, audit or FP&A. Follow it for intake, evidence, CV shape, keywords and screening answers.

## Titles and adjacent roles

- Common titles: Accounts Payable or Receivable Clerk, Bookkeeper, Staff Accountant, Management Accountant, Financial Accountant, Payroll Specialist, Financial Analyst, FP&A Analyst, Internal or External Auditor, Tax Accountant, Finance Manager.
- Realistic moves after a layoff: accounts clerk to staff accountant or payroll; accountant to financial analyst or FP&A; audit to internal audit, risk or compliance; accountant to finance systems or business analysis (if ERP work is real); industry to practice (or the reverse).
- Search the person's title plus adjacent ones. Ads differ by country, sector and whether the firm is a practice or in industry.

## Evidence sources

- Close and reporting: month-end close days, reconciliations owned, reports produced, variance analysis, budget and forecast accuracy.
- Process work: automation of journals or reports, ERP or Excel models built, controls introduced, audit findings cleared.
- Operational numbers: invoices processed per month, payment accuracy, days payable or receivable, payroll runs and headcount covered.
- Public: a fictional or public-company model, a method write-up. Private: client financials, employer ledgers, budgets, payroll and employee pay data, audit workpapers, tax details. Never include client financials; describe scale in bands ("a 200-employee business") and results as percentages or days saved.

## Intake questions

1. Which area was the main work? (answer as it is: payables, receivables, general ledger, payroll, audit, tax, FP&A)
2. Which direction do you want?
   a) **Same role and level (Recommended: strongest evidence)**
   b) Move to a neighbouring finance role (for example accountant to FP&A)
   c) Practice to industry, or the reverse
   d) Other: type it
3. Which results can you share? (pick any)
   a) **Close time, accuracy or process savings (Recommended: most asked for)**
   b) Audit or compliance outcomes
   c) Models, reports or automation built
   d) Other: type it
4. Which systems have you used? (answer as it is: ERP, Excel level, payroll and accounting packages, years)
5. Do you hold or study for a professional qualification, and from which body? (answer as it is: name, country of the body, stage, or none)

## CV shape

- Order: header, summary, skills, experience, qualifications, education.
- Summary leads with years, the area, the systems and one measurable result. Put the professional qualification near the top if the person holds one.
- Bullets, action then result then how:
  - "Cut month-end close from 8 to 5 working days for a fictional 3-entity group by standardising reconciliations."
  - "Processed 1,200 supplier invoices a month at 99.5% accuracy in SAP."
  - "Built a rolling 12-month forecast in Excel that brought variance to budget under 3%."
- Show accuracy, timeliness and scale in bands. Never invent a number.

## Keywords and credentials

- Keywords from the ad: month-end close, reconciliation, accounts payable, general ledger, financial reporting, IFRS, GAAP, variance analysis, budgeting, forecasting, payroll, VAT or sales tax, audit, ERP, SAP, Xero, QuickBooks, advanced Excel.
- Qualifications depend on country. CPA is licensed by national or state bodies: AICPA and state boards for the US (aicpa-cima.com), CPA Australia (cpaaustralia.com.au), CPA Canada for Canada. ACCA (accaglobal.com) is often named in UK, Irish, Gulf, African and Asian ads; check the target ads. CIMA is offered through the CIMA and AICPA site (aicpa-cima.com). An ad may name one of these as required or preferred, or accept part-qualified candidates. Check the ad and the country; a credential from one country may not be recognised in another.

## Portfolio and work samples

- Not expected. FP&A and finance systems ads sometimes ask for a model or Excel test.
- Build any sample from a fictional or public company; never use real client or employer figures.

## Screening questions

- "Years of accounting experience": count roles where the ledger, payables, receivables or reporting was the main job.
- "Experience with SAP, Xero, QuickBooks, Excel": answer from `experience.items`, with years.
- "Are you qualified or part-qualified (ACCA, CPA, CIMA)?": answer as it is: stage and body, or none; never round up "part-qualified" to "qualified".
- "Right to work" and licence status: facts; answer as it is.

## Answer-bank experience keys

Set `"role_family": "finance-accounting"`. Collect lowercase keys with years or true/false: `accounts payable`, `accounts receivable`, `general ledger`, `month-end close`, `reconciliation`, `payroll`, `fp&a`, `budgeting`, `ifrs`, `us gaap`, `sap`, `xero`, `quickbooks`, `excel`, `audit`, `acca`, `cpa`, `cima`, each only when fully qualified; a part-qualified stage goes in `questions` as the candidate's own answer.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Naming clients or including client or employer financial figures.
- Stating a qualification the person has not completed, or one from another country without saying which.
- Listing duties ("responsible for reconciliations") with no result.
- Mixing up the CIMA and CMA designations.
