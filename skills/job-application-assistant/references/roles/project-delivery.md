# Role profile: project and delivery management

Load this when the person manages projects or delivery: project and programme management, Scrum Master, delivery lead. Follow it for intake, evidence, CV shape, keywords and screening answers.

## Titles and adjacent roles

- Common titles: Project Manager, Technical Project Manager, Delivery Manager, Scrum Master, Agile Coach, Programme Manager, Project Coordinator, PMO Analyst, Release Manager.
- Realistic moves after a layoff: project manager to delivery manager or Scrum Master (and back); Scrum Master to product owner or agile coach; project coordinator to project manager; project manager to business analyst, product owner or programme manager; PMO analyst to project manager.
- Search the person's title plus "delivery", "scrum" and "programme". The same work is titled differently in IT, construction and consulting; keep to the sector the evidence is in.

## Evidence sources

- Plans and reports: project charters, schedules, RAID logs, status reports, retrospectives and lessons learned. Quote dates, budgets and scope.
- Tool exports: Jira, Azure DevOps, MS Project, Smartsheet, Asana. Read velocity, burndown, on-time delivery, budget variance.
- Outcome records: go-live notices, sign-off emails, benefits reports, stakeholder praise.
- Public: a talk, a blog post on agile practice, certificates. Private under an NDA: client names, budgets, contracts, internal reports. Describe the sector and give budget as a range.

## Intake questions

1. Which delivery style is the main strength? (answer as it is: waterfall, agile or Scrum, hybrid, programme or PMO)
2. Which direction do you want?
   a) **Same role and level (Recommended: strongest evidence)**
   b) Project manager to Scrum Master, delivery manager or programme manager
   c) Move to product owner or business analyst
   d) Other: type it
3. What scale can you state? (answer as it is: team size, budget range, duration, number of stakeholders)
4. Which delivery certificates do you hold, such as PMP, CAPM, PRINCE2, PSM or CSM? (answer as it is: name and level, or none)
5. Where is the proof of delivery?
   a) **Project reports or a tool export (Recommended: shows scale)**
   b) Sign-off or go-live records
   c) Other: type it

## CV shape

- Order: header, summary, skills, experience, certifications, education.
- Summary leads with years delivering, the method (agile, waterfall, hybrid), the typical scale (team, budget, duration) and the sector.
- Bullets, scale then action then result:
  - "Delivered a 9-month CRM migration for 120 users, 6% under a mid six-figure budget, across 4 teams at Example Retail."
  - "Coached 3 Scrum teams to a stable cadence, cutting carry-over stories from 35% to 10% over 6 sprints."
  - "Ran weekly RAID reviews that closed 22 of 25 risks before go-live."
- Always give budget, team size, duration or on-time result. Never invent a number.

## Keywords and credentials

- Keywords from the ad: stakeholder management, risk management, budget, scope, schedule, Agile, Scrum, Kanban, Jira, MS Project, RAID, PMO, change management, sprint planning, retrospectives.
- Many delivery ads mention a certificate. Names seen: PMP and CAPM (PMI), PRINCE2 (axelos.com and PeopleCert), PSM (Scrum.org), CSM (Scrum Alliance). Some ads require one, others prefer it, many name none; check the target ads.

## Portfolio and work samples

- Not expected. A redacted plan, status report or retrospective format can help if the interview asks.

## Screening questions

- "Years of project management experience": count roles leading delivery; coordination support is separate.
- "Largest budget or team managed": answer from the bank; give the real figure or a range.
- "Do you hold PMP, PRINCE2 or CSM?": answer from the bank, true or false; "in progress" only if booked.
- "Agile, waterfall or hybrid?": answer from the methods in the bank.

## Answer-bank experience keys

Collect lowercase keys with years or true/false: `project management`, `scrum`, `kanban`, `agile`, `waterfall`, `risk management`, `budget management`, `stakeholder management`, `jira`, `ms project`, `azure devops`, `smartsheet`, `pmo`, `pmp`, `capm`, `prince2 foundation`, `psm i`, `csm`. Store a credential at the level held, as named on the certificate (`prince2 practitioner`, `psm ii`); a question naming a higher level is then asked, not answered.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Writing duties ("tracked tasks") with no scale or result.
- Taking credit for a team's delivery with no named decision of the person's own.
- Listing a certificate that has lapsed or is only planned.
- Naming the client or exact contract value under an NDA.
