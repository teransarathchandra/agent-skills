# Role profile: sales and account management

Load this when the person works in sales, business development, account management or pre-sales. Follow it for intake, evidence, CV shape, keywords and screening answers.

## Titles and adjacent roles

- Common titles: Sales Development Representative, Account Executive, Business Development Manager, Account Manager, Key Account Manager, Inside Sales Representative, Sales Engineer, Solutions Consultant, Customer Success Manager.
- Realistic moves after a layoff: SDR to account executive or account manager; account executive to account management or customer success (less quota pressure); sales to pre-sales, sales engineer or partnerships; field sales to inside sales; sales to marketing or sales operations.
- Search the person's title plus adjacent ones. Ads split by motion (new business versus farming), deal size and sector; ask which the person did.

## Evidence sources

- Quota and pipeline: percent of target, ranking on the team, pipeline created, win rate, average deal size, sales cycle length, renewal and expansion rates.
- CRM reports: Salesforce or HubSpot exports, forecast accuracy, activity records.
- Awards and recognition: president's club, top-rep rankings, written praise.
- Public or shareable: percent of target ("112% of quota"), rank ("3rd of 14 reps"), award names, sector and deal size band. Private: absolute revenue, named customers and deals, pricing and discounting, pipeline values, commission figures. Quota attainment and pipeline numbers are sensitive; share ratios and rankings, and keep absolute amounts private unless the employer has published them.

## Intake questions

1. Which sales motion was the main work? (answer as it is: new business, account management, inside sales, field sales, pre-sales, channel)
2. Which direction do you want?
   a) **Same role and level (Recommended: strongest evidence)**
   b) Sales to account management or customer success
   c) Sales to pre-sales, sales engineering or sales operations
   d) Other: type it
3. Which results can you share? (pick any)
   a) **Percent of target and team ranking (Recommended: safest to share)**
   b) Win rate and sales cycle
   c) Renewal or expansion rate
   d) Other: type it
4. Which CRM and sales tools have you used? (answer as it is: names and years)
5. Do you hold a Salesforce or HubSpot credential, or a sales methodology certificate? (answer as it is: name, or none)

## CV shape

- Order: header, summary, skills, experience, certifications, education.
- Summary leads with years, the motion, the deal or customer type and one ratio result.
- Bullets, action then result then how:
  - "Reached 118% of annual quota in 2025, ranking 2nd of 12 account executives at a fictional SaaS firm."
  - "Grew a book of 25 mid-market accounts, lifting renewal rate from 84% to 93% through quarterly reviews."
  - "Built 3 outbound sequences that doubled booked demos per rep."
- Use percent and rank. State the sector and customer size without naming customers. Never invent a number.

## Keywords and credentials

- Keywords from the ad: quota, pipeline, prospecting, lead generation, closing, negotiation, CRM, Salesforce, forecasting, account planning, upsell, renewal, solution selling, MEDDIC, discovery.
- Salesforce credentials are on Trailhead (trailhead.salesforce.com); HubSpot Academy (academy.hubspot.com) offers sales training and certificates. Some ads mention them; most sales ads care more about attainment and track record. Check the target ads; no credential is needed unless ads show it.

## Portfolio and work samples

- Not expected. Pre-sales and sales engineering ads sometimes ask for a demo or case study.
- Build any sample from a fictional customer; never reuse a real proposal or pricing.

## Screening questions

- "Years of B2B sales experience": count quota-carrying or account-owning roles.
- "Have you met or exceeded quota?": answer from the bank; give the percent and period if the person is willing, otherwise "yes" or "no" truthfully.
- "Experience with Salesforce or HubSpot": answer from `experience.items`.
- "Average deal size": ask the person; offer a band if the exact figure is confidential, and say it is a band.
- "Willing to travel": the person's own answer.

## Answer-bank experience keys

Set `"role_family": "sales-account-management"`. Collect lowercase keys with years or true/false: `b2b sales`, `outbound prospecting`, `account management`, `pipeline management`, `salesforce`, `hubspot crm`, `negotiation`, `forecasting`, `solution selling`, `pre-sales`, `meddic`, `quota attainment`, `salesforce certified`.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Publishing absolute revenue, named deals, pricing or pipeline values the employer treats as confidential.
- Claiming quota attainment for a period with no quota, or rounding a miss into a win.
- Naming customers in bullets.
- Listing activity ("made 80 calls a day") with no outcome.
