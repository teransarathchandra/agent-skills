# Contract checklist (a draft - have a lawyer review your template once)
- Scope, deliverables, acceptance criteria and an acceptance window
- Price: fixed build plus an optional monthly maintenance retainer; currency; payment terms; late fee
- IP assigns to the client on full payment; your background IP (skills, templates, prompts, tools) stays yours with a licence to the client
- AI-use disclosure and the agreed tier (see ai-use-policy.md); AI provider listed as a subprocessor where used
- Warranty limited to reasonable review and testing; liability cap
- Confidentiality and data handling (no production data on your machine unless agreed)
- Change requests priced separately; termination terms
- If you are also employed: check your employment contract for outside-work, IP and conflict-of-interest clauses first
